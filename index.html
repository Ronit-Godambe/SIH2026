Dynamic Forecast of
Expected Time of Arrival

Can we move from simply knowing where a train is now to continuously forecasting where it will actually reach next?

01 — The Problem

Train delays are dynamic. A train may be running late now, but congestion, signals, weather, sectional running times and network conditions can make its future delay increase or decrease.

Current state

Where is the train? How late is it right now?

Future state

When will it actually reach each upcoming station?

The challenge

Continuously update that forecast as new events occur.

02 — What Railways Already Has

Indian Railways already has systems for real-time train information and operational management. RTIS provides train-position information, while systems such as COA support railway control operations and forecasting. NTES exposes train-running information to passengers.

RTIS

Real-time train positioning and movement information.

COA

Control-office operational information and train movement management.

NTES

Passenger-facing train running and enquiry information.

The opportunity: add a predictive intelligence layer that continuously combines current state, historical behaviour and changing network conditions.

03 — What Are They Expecting From Us?

Inputs

Train location · speed · current delay · signals · historical section times · weather · downstream congestion.

Prediction

Use statistical / ML forecasting to estimate remaining travel time and future station ETAs.

Dynamic updates

When conditions change, recalculate the ETA instead of keeping a static prediction.

06 — What We Are Doing

We are proposing a predictive layer over existing railway information systems. In a real deployment, live operational feeds could replace the simulated inputs used here.

1. Data ingestion

Collect train state, timetable, historical running patterns and network conditions.

2. Feature engineering

Convert raw operational data into useful prediction features for each train and section.

3. ETA model

Predict remaining travel time, then calculate ETA for upcoming stations.

4. Continuous update

Recalculate whenever a meaningful change in the train or network is detected.
